Prototype notes

Try: tap ⋯ on a song for the context menu; swipe a song row left; Edit in a playlist (drag ≡ to reorder); touch-and-hold or right-click a folder; tap a lyric line to seek.

Placeholders: SF Symbols are approximated with inline SVG. Artwork is generated, not real covers. Catalog, counts, and lyrics are invented (only “Midnight City / M83” comes from the brief). The Songs list shows a sample of the stated 1,434 songs.

Design decisions: Tapping a song starts playback and shows the mini-player (as in Apple Music); tap the mini-player to open Now Playing. Now Playing is full-screen for height. Volume slider omitted (hardware buttons).
